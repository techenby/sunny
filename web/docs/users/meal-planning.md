# Meal Planning

The meal plan shows what the household is eating each day of the week. Most
weeks look alike, so meals come from a **weekly template**, and you change
individual meals on specific dates when plans change.

The meal plan belongs to the current team. Everyone on the team can view and
change it.

## Meal slots

Each day is split into meal slots. New teams start with **Breakfast**,
**Lunch**, and **Dinner**. To change them:

1. Open **Meal Plan** from the main sidebar.
2. Select **Weekly template**, then **Meal slots**.
3. Rename a slot in its field, use the arrow buttons to reorder slots, or add a
   new one, such as **Snack**.

Removing a slot clears it from the template and from upcoming days. Days that
have already passed keep what was planned in it.

## Set up the weekly template

The template lists the meals for each weekday, such as tacos every Tuesday.

1. Open **Meal Plan**, then select **Weekly template**.
2. Select **Add** in a slot on a weekday.
3. Start typing to pick one of the team's recipes, or type anything else, such
   as "Leftovers" or "Pizza night". Select **Add**.

A slot can hold more than one meal, such as a main and a side. Use the menu on
a meal to move it up or down or remove it.

## Change a meal on a specific date

On the **Meal Plan** page, use the arrows to move between weeks. Today is
highlighted.

Adding, removing, or reordering meals on a date changes only that slot on that
date. The template and every other date stay the same. Changed slots show an
undo button; select it to put the template's meals back.

To skip a meal on one date, remove everything in that slot.

## Meal history

When a day ends, Sunny saves that day's meals, so changing the template later
doesn't rewrite the past. A recipe's page shows the last date it was on the
meal plan under **Last Made**.

## Add ingredients to a shopping list

1. On the **Meal Plan** page, select **Shopping list**.
2. Choose the dates to shop for. Sunny starts with the rest of the week you are
   viewing.
3. Untick any ingredients you already have. A recipe planned more than once is
   listed once.
4. Choose a shopping list and select **Add to list**.

Ingredients are added exactly as they are written in each recipe. Meals typed
as free text have no ingredients, so they are not included. You need at least
one list of the **Shopping** type; see [Lists and Checklists](/docs/users/checklists).
