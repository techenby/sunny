# Routines

Routines are repeating checklists for activities that happen on a schedule,
such as getting ready in the morning, weekly chores, or monthly home
maintenance.

Routines belong to the current team and are shared with everyone on that team.
Assigning a routine to a person helps the household see who it belongs to, but
does not make it private or prevent another team member from updating it.

## Create a routine in the main app

1. Open **Routines** from the main sidebar.
2. Select **Add Routine**.
3. Enter a name and assign the routine to the whole **Household** or to a
   specific team member.
4. Choose **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening**, or **Anytime**. This controls
   how routines are ordered on the kiosk.
5. Choose how often the routine repeats:
   - **Daily** runs every day.
   - **Weekly** runs on the selected weekdays.
   - **Monthly** runs on the selected day of the month. If that date does not
     exist in a shorter month, the routine runs on the last day of that month.
6. Choose the start date, leave **Active** enabled, and select **Create**.
7. Add the steps that should be completed.

Use the arrow buttons to reorder steps, edit a step directly in its field, or
use the trash button to remove it.

The menu on each routine card lets you edit its schedule, open its steps,
pause or resume it, or delete it. Paused routines stop appearing for today and
future dates on the kiosk. Previously generated dates remain available when
browsing kiosk history.

The main app is where you manage the routine and its steps. Due steps are
completed from a kiosk.

## Complete a routine on a kiosk

After [setting up a kiosk display](/docs/users/kiosk/getting-started), select
**Routines** in the kiosk sidebar. The screen shows one column for every active
routine due on the selected date, ordered by time of day. Each column shows
the assignee, schedule period, completed-step count, and progress.

- Tap a step to mark it complete; tap it again to undo it.
- The name of the account that completed a step appears beside it.
- Use the left and right arrows to review another day, or select **Today** to
  return to the current date.
- Completion is stored separately for each date. Completing today's routine
  does not complete yesterday's or tomorrow's copy.

If nothing is scheduled for the selected date, the kiosk displays
**Nothing to do**.
